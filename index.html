Takeaways

  1. 01Static: 1.07–1.6× faster secondary-ray traversal than state-of-the-art compressed 8-ary AABB BVH (BVH8) — Sheep: 9.3×.
  2. 02Dynamic: SOBB refit ≈ 1.5× an AABB refit — the trace advantage survives.
  3. 03Modest costs: +14–26% build, +5% memory, 7–15 bits/node.
